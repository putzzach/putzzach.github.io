Curiosity is a small engine for getting unstuck. It turns "I don't know" from a dead end into a direction: What is that? How does it work? What would happen if I tried?

You don't need a grand plan to follow a question. Read one page, take one thing apart, or ask someone how they got into what they do. A tiny detour can change what you notice next.

The point isn't to collect answers as fast as possible. It's to stay interested long enough to find a better question.
