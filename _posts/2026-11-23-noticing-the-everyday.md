Most days are made of details too small to make the recap: the light shifting across a wall, a familiar route sounding different after rain, the extra minute someone takes to explain something carefully.

Pausing to notice doesn't make an ordinary day extraordinary. It just lets the day be more than the list of things you had to get through.

Maybe attention is its own kind of bookmark. It marks a moment so you can come back to it later, even if nothing else happened.
